PENNSYLVANIA CIVIL LITIGATION

There's more to an Answer than saying no.

When someone files a civil lawsuit, the defendant has an opportunity to respond.

Sometimes, the defendant denies what the plaintiff says happened. Other times, the defendant raises an affirmative defense. This means the defendant has another legal reason the plaintiff may not be able to win the case.

Denial: “I never borrowed that money.”

Affirmative Defense: “I borrowed the money, but I already paid it back.”

In Pennsylvania, affirmative defenses are generally included in a section of the defendant's pleading called New Matter.
